How 29 LPA Becomes ₹1,86,517 In-Hand

Your CTC (Cost to Company) of ₹29 Lisn't the same as what lands in your bank account. Employers split CTC into fixed pay, employer contributions you never receive as cash, and one-time-on-exit benefits like gratuity. Here's the typical breakdown:

  1. Basic salary is set at roughly 40% of CTC — ₹11,60,000 per year, or ₹96,667/month.
  2. HRA (House Rent Allowance) is typically 50% of basic — ₹48,333/month, partly or fully tax-exempt under the old regime if you pay rent.
  3. Employer PF contribution (₹4,798/month) and gratuity (₹4,650/month) are part of CTC but aren't paid to you monthly — gratuity is only payable after 5+ years of service.
  4. From your gross salary of ₹2,32,219/month, your own PF contribution (₹11,600) and income tax (₹34,102) are deducted, leaving your net in-hand pay.

Old Regime vs. New Regime

At this CTC, the new tax regime works out cheaper by ₹2,49,600per year — though the old regime's actual benefit depends heavily on deductions like 80C, HRA exemption, and home loan interest, which aren't available under the new regime.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the in-hand salary for 29 LPA per month?

For a CTC of ₹29 L per year, the estimated in-hand salary is ₹1,86,517 per month under the new tax regime, after PF and income tax deductions. Actual take-home may vary based on your employer's specific salary structure.

How much tax will I pay on 29 LPA?

Under the new tax regime, the estimated annual income tax on a gross salary derived from 29 LPA CTC is ₹4,09,228. This includes the Section 87A rebate where applicable.

Is 29 LPA a good salary in India?

This depends heavily on your city, experience level, and industry. 29 LPA translates to roughly ₹1,86,517 in-hand per month, which you can compare against typical living costs in your city.
